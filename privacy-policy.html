This policy explains how personal information may be handled when you visit this portfolio website or contact Atif Noorul Hasan.

If you contact me by email, LinkedIn, GitHub, or another external platform, I may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, organization, project requirements, and message content.

The current website does not intentionally use its own advertising cookies or tracking scripts. Basic technical information may still be processed by the website hosting provider to operate, secure, and deliver the site.

If analytics tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated and any legally required cookie or consent controls will be implemented.

Some pages may contain or later include embedded Power BI reports, YouTube videos, GitHub repositories, LinkedIn links, or other third-party content. I do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary with service providers supporting communication or website operation, when required by law, or when necessary to protect legal rights and security.

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, professional record-keeping, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. 7. Your Choices and Rights
