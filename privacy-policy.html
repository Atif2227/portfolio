Privacy Policy

How AtifData handles information shared through this website

AtifData is an independent data modernization and analytics consultancy led by Atif Noorul Hasan. This policy explains the information the website may receive when you contact AtifData.

Information you provide

When you use the consultation or contact form, you may provide information such as your name, work email, company or organization, country, project context and message. Please do not submit confidential, sensitive, regulated or production data through the website form.

How the information is used

Information you submit is used to review your enquiry, respond to you, understand the business or data problem you want to discuss, and determine whether AtifData may be able to help.

Sharing and selling

AtifData does not sell personal information submitted through this website. Information may be handled by service providers that support website hosting, email or form delivery where those services are used.

Retention

Enquiry information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant business correspondence, or meet applicable record-keeping obligations.

External links

The website may link to external services such as LinkedIn, GitHub and YouTube. Those services operate under their own privacy practices and policies.

Your request

If you have a question about information you submitted through this website, use the contact details provided on the website to request access, correction or deletion where applicable.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as AtifData's website, tools and business operations develop. The version published on this page is the current website policy.
