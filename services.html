Services

A practical path from data problems to reliable analytics

Engage AtifData at the point that matches your current need—assessment, modernization, analytics implementation or ongoing managed support.

Data & Analytics Assessment

Know what to fix before investing in technology.

Review your current systems, reporting processes and data-quality concerns. You receive a written findings summary, a recommended target architecture and a prioritized implementation roadmap.

Before work begins, we agree the systems and business questions in scope, the timeline and fee. The review requires access to relevant documentation and discussions with system owners and reporting users. Any sample data or system access is agreed in advance. Implementation is scoped separately.

Data Modernization & Analytics Consulting

Build a reliable foundation for analytics.

Integrate your data sources, automate processing and build reliable data models and reporting. The architecture fits your environment, using Microsoft Fabric, SQL Server, Python and Power BI where appropriate.

The agreed scope defines the data sources, deliverables and acceptance criteria, including documentation and handover.

Industry Analytics Solutions

Analytics adapted to your business questions.

Use frameworks such as Commerce Intelligence as a starting point for your own reporting. KPI definitions and data models are adapted to your systems and validated against your business rules.

Managed Data & Analytics

Keep analytics reliable after implementation.

Ongoing support can cover pipeline monitoring, data-quality checks, issue investigation and agreed report or KPI enhancements.

Your support agreement defines the systems covered, service hours, response expectations and the allowance for enhancements. Larger changes and new integrations are scoped separately.
